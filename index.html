Our Story

Naandhi was born from a chance encounter in a college classroom. Two strangers with a shared passion for entrepreneurship found themselves in the same room—one for patent work, the other for research and development. When their professor mentioned the opportunity to start a company through the college incubation center, a spark was ignited.

As they began to talk, they discovered their thoughts aligned perfectly. Both had observed a critical pain point in agriculture: while drone fertilization technology existed, it wasn't easily accessible to farmers. The current system forced farmers to wait 4-5 days, making multiple calls just to get a drone to their field.

This realization led to the founding of Naandhi—a company dedicated to making agricultural drone services accessible, affordable, and efficient for farmers in Andhra Pradesh. Starting with drone fertilization services, our vision expanded to include promoting organic farming practices and providing comprehensive agricultural support. Today, we're building a platform that bridges traditional farming wisdom with modern technology, creating a sustainable future for agriculture in India.
